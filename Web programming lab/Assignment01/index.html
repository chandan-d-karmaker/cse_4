About Miku

Hatsune Miku is a virtual singer made by Crypton Future Media and first released on August 31, 2007. Her name means "the first sound of the future". She is a voice synthesizer, so anyone can write a song and have Miku sing it. Because of that, fans around the world have created thousands of songs, pictures and videos with her.

What Miku Is Known For

  • Pop and electronic music
  • Live holographic concerts
  • Fan-made songs, art and videos
  • Collaborations with artists, brands and anime
  • Her favorite food: leeks (negi)
